
# To-Do-List

## A Simple To-Do Website!

**Just Add It!** - A Daily Focus Planner to capture and manage your tasks efficiently.

---

## 📖 How to Use

### Step 1: **Add a Task**
- Type your task in the text field under "Quick Capture"
- Click **"Just Add It!"** button (or press Enter)
- Your task appears in the "Your focus queue" section below

### Step 2: **Complete a Task**
- Click the **✓ (check)** button next to a task to mark it as complete
- The task text will show as strikethrough
- Completed tasks are removed from view after the animation

### Step 3: **Delete a Task**
- Click the **🗑️ (trash)** button to permanently delete a task
- The task disappears with a falling animation

### Step 4: **Change Theme**
- Click the theme buttons in the top-right corner
  - **Blue dot** = Standard (Dark Blue)
  - **Yellow dot** = Light (Bright)
  - **Black dot** = Darker (Very Dark)

### Step 5: **Monitor Your Progress**
- See your task count in the "Today's List" section
- Check the current date and time displayed above the task list

---

## ✨ Features

* **User-friendly Interface** - Clean, minimal design for focused task management
* **Local Storage Support** - Your tasks are saved in your browser (persists after refresh)
* **Real-time Date & Time** - Displays current date and time
* **Responsive Design** - Works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
* **Multiple Themes** - Choose between Standard, Light, and Darker themes
* **Task Animations** - Smooth check, delete, and fall animations for visual feedback
* **Empty State** - Helpful message when no tasks exist
* **Task Summary** - See total number of tasks at a glance
